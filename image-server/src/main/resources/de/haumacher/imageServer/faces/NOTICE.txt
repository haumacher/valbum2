Third-party models bundled with VAlbum (issue #124)
===================================================

The two ONNX models in this directory are redistributed unchanged, byte for byte,
from the OpenCV Model Zoo (https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo).  Both carry a
permissive licence that allows redistribution; the licence texts are reproduced
below.  Neither model was trained, modified or re-exported by this project.

              Robotics for Society)

  Why the 2022mar revision and not the newer 2023mar one: the newer graph needs
  an OpenCV DNN of 4.8 or later, and the bundled org.bytedeco:opencv of the
  JavaCPP presets 1.5.8 this server builds against is OpenCV 4.6.0, which
  refuses to load it ("eltwise_layer.cpp:251: inputs[vecIdx][j] ==
  inputs[i][j]").  When the JavaCPP presets are upgraded, exchanging the file and
  the model stamp in FaceDetection is all that is needed -- every album is
  re-indexed by itself, because the stamp is part of what .vacache/faces.json is
  keyed by.

What the models are used for
----------------------------

Detection and the embedding of a detected face, on the server, on the preview
rendition of a photograph and never on the original.  The embeddings are a cache
in the album's .vacache and never leave the server: what goes on the wire is a
box and a cluster identifier, see de.haumacher.imageServer.faces.FaceIndex.
